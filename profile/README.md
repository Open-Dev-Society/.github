## 🤝 Contributing

1. Pick a project above and read its README.
2. Find an open issue — [`good first issue`](https://github.com/search?q=org%3AOpen-Dev-Society+is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22&type=issues) is the place to start.
3. Comment to claim it, so nobody duplicates your work.
4. Open a pull request.

Code is one way in, not the only one. Writing, docs, design, teaching, translation, video, events and bug reports all count — the [Contributing Guidelines](https://github.com/Open-Dev-Society/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) cover each path in detail.

**Bringing your own project?** You stay the creator and lead maintainer. We add contributors, review, and visibility.

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Can I showcase projects under ODS as my own work?</b></summary>

Absolutely. Projects you create or contribute to remain yours. List them on your resume, portfolio, and LinkedIn — ODS exists to support your growth, not claim your achievements.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What happens to my project if I transfer it to ODS?</b></summary>

You retain full credit as creator and can continue as lead maintainer. We provide community support, contributors, and visibility — you keep control over direction and decisions.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I lose ownership rights?</b></summary>

No. ODS is a platform, not a company claiming IP. You always own your work and can showcase it as yours.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I leave or take my project back?</b></summary>

Yes. If you ever want to move your project out of ODS, we'll support that decision. No lock-in, no penalties — just community support when you need it.
</details>
